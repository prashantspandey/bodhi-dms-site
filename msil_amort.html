Maruti Suzuki (MSIL) — Railway Freight Bifurcation

How MSIL pays IVC for railway dispatches: two-component billing structure

Source: IVC.dbo.CNMaster · Verified from DB · Oct 2024–Oct 2025
What is the structure?
For every car transported by railway, MSIL raises two billing lines to IVC — not one. The regular Maruti CN covers road transport rates. The Rail-tagged CN (ConsignorName ending in -Rail) is an additional overlay with exactly two components: a variable road-delivery charge and a fixed amortisation component.

Component 1 — Variable

DB field: Freight
₹1,828 – ₹15,549 / car

Road delivery distance from the railway unloading yard to the dealer. Every car in the same rake gets a different amount depending on how far its dealer is from the railway yard. Calculated per km.

Component 2 — Fixed Amortisation

DB field: SurCharge
₹1,042 – ₹1,659 / car

Incentive for every car in a train load — identical for all cars in the same rake. This is Maruti's contribution to IVC's cost of owning and operating the IVCLL dedicated automobile rakes. Revised periodically via rate contract.

Per-car billing on a Rail CN:
Total per car = Freight (variable, km-based) + SurCharge (fixed amortisation)
Example (Jul 2025, car going to dealer 2,825 km from Manesar):
₹15,028 (Freight, road delivery) + ₹1,659 (SurCharge, amortisation) = ₹16,687/car

What Does "Amortisation" Mean Here?

Economic logic

IVC owns 12 dedicated automobile rakes (IVCLL-01 to IVCLL-12). Each rake costs several crores to procure and maintain. These are dedicated to MSIL routes — so MSIL agrees to pay a per-car amortisation charge on top of the normal road rate, effectively sharing the fixed cost of the rake fleet.

This is why it is called an incentive — it incentivises IVC to invest in dedicated rakes for MSIL, making railway transport economically viable for both parties.

How it flows in the DB

CNMaster (MSIL Rail CN)
├─ txtCN_ConsignorName = 'Maruti Suzuki India Ltd. (MNSR-Rail)'
├─ Freight = 7,014 ← variable (road distance)
├─ SurCharge = 1,659 ← fixed (amortisation)
├─ SubTotal = Freight + SurCharge = 8,673
└─ FreightTotal = SubTotal × 1.18 (GST)
Historical context: This mechanism has been running since October 2019 (first tag: "Maruti Suzuki India Ltd. (Manesar-Rail)" @ ₹1,416/car). It paused during COVID (rate dropped to ₹678/car in Mar 2020), was restored in Dec 2021 (₹1,147/car), and the current MNSR-Rail / GGN-Rail tags started in January 2025.
