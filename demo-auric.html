Phase 1 · Week 1 · your admin + our team, together

Foundation: teach the system your dealership

Nothing transactional yet. First the system learns who you are, who works for you, and who is allowed to do what — because every booking, invoice and voucher that follows will reference this skeleton.

Company & branches Admin

Your legal entity with GSTIN, then every showroom, workshop and stockyard as a branch. Why first: every record — a receipt, a job card, an approval — is stamped with its branch, so branch-wise P&L works from day one.

Users & the rights matrix Admin enforced server-side

Every employee gets a login. Then you tick, per user, exactly which modules they may open — a sales DSE never sees payroll; a cashier never sees discounts. Unlike your current setup, this is enforced by the server, not just hidden menus.

The approval matrix Dealer principal + Accounts head

You decide: refunds above ₹25,000 need the GM; discounts above scheme limit need two levels; petty cash needs the accountant. This is the maker–checker spine — from now on, nothing money-shaped posts on one person's say-so, and the maker of a record can never approve it himself.

Masters: models, financiers, insurers, price lists Sales back-office

Your Maruti model/variant/colour catalogue with GST rates, the financiers you work with (and their payout rates), insurers, payment modes, discount schemes with maximum limits. Why: when a cost sheet is built next week, every line comes from these masters — no typed-in prices, no "adjusted" discounts.

Credential vault Admin encrypted

Your GSTIN e-invoice credentials, MSIL dealer code for the GD-FDI feed, WhatsApp/SMS provider — stored encrypted, per dealership. The workflows are already built; when a credential lands, that integration simply switches on. No code, no vendor wait.

why this order

Skip this week and every later entry becomes a free-text guess. Do it right and the system starts refusing bad data on your behalf — wrong branch, over-limit discount, unauthorized user — automatically, forever.

Phase 2 · Week 2 · bulk imports, not typing

Opening position: start where your old books end

Now the system learns what you own and what you're owed. Everything here is a CSV/Excel import with per-row validation — you see exactly which rows loaded and which need fixing.

Vehicle stock Stock in-charge

Every chassis in your yard — VIN, model, colour, purchase date, purchase value, which stockyard. Why: your very first invoice next week must pull a real chassis, and stock aging starts counting from these dates (you'll see why that matters at allotment).

Ledger opening balances Accounts head / your CA

Customer and vendor ledgers with closing balances from your current system, bank balances, group structure. Why: your trial balance on Bodhi day one equals your trial balance on the old system's last day — the auditor's first question, answered before it's asked.

Spare parts stock Parts manager

Part numbers, HSN, MRP, current quantity and minimum levels. The minimums power automatic reorder suggestions later.

Employees & salary structures HR

Staff master with department, branch, CTC and component-wise salary structure, PF/ESI numbers. Payroll goes live in month 3, but attendance can start recording from today.

Insurance policy book Insurance desk

Your existing policies with expiry dates. Why now: the renewal engine immediately starts building your renewal pipeline — the easiest revenue you're currently leaking.

Real reference: for Motorcraft Sales Pvt. Ltd (MAPL), we migrated 56 million rows across 893 legacy tables — invoices, ledgers, 90,000 policies, 47,000 payslips — and the opening trial balance tied to ₹2,134 crore before go-live.

Phase 3 · Desk 1 · the sales floor — live from week 3

Follow one car: enquiry to delivery

This is a normal Tuesday at Auric, one month in. A customer walks into your showroom. Watch what each person touches — and what the system does around them.

Enquiry & quotation DSE

Your DSE logs the walk-in — name, phone, model interest, budget. Follow-ups get a timeline; no lead lives in a personal diary anymore. One click builds a quotation: ex-showroom from the price master, insurance, RTO, accessories — a printed on-road price in the customer's hand in two minutes.

Booking DSE MSIL feed auto-syncs

Customer says yes → booking with advance receipt. Bookings from the MSIL GD-FDI feed flow in automatically and de-duplicate — your Maruti order book and your DMS stop being two different truths.

Cost sheet DSE → Accounts checker maker–checker

The deal's financial DNA: ex-showroom, insurance, RTO, accessories, warranty, TCS, every discount component. The DSE makes it, accounts checks it. Receipts recorded against it auto-reduce the balance; it closes itself at zero.

Discounts DSE requests · manager approves limit enforced

Consumer offer, exchange bonus, corporate — each requested by component. If the total crosses the scheme's maximum, the server refuses it until the configured approver signs off. At MAPL's scale this single control is worth lakhs a month.

Allotment Stock in-charge FIFO enforced

Which chassis goes to this customer? The system recommends the oldest matching stock first — skip an older chassis and it demands an approval. Your stock aging stops silently rotting in the yard.

Invoice Billing GST + e-invoice IRN

One click: GST splits computed from the model master (28% + cess, CGST/SGST vs IGST), the chassis flips to SOLD, the booking to INVOICED — and the e-invoice IRN with QR generates against your GSTIN. Cancellation after IRN? Blocked, with an approval trail.

RTO & delivery RTO desk

Eight registration milestones tracked per car, Vahan reconciliation by chassis, fees logged. Delivery day: gate pass printed, the customer drives out, and your dashboard already shows this sale in this branch's numbers.

If it goes sideways: refunds & cancellations Anyone requests · matrix approves

Booking cancelled? The refund computes booking + additional − cancellation charges, routes through the approval matrix, and only on final approval posts the voucher and releases the chassis back to stock. No verbal refunds, ever.

what changed for you

Every rupee of this deal — advance, discount, refund, payout — moved through a gate someone else approved. Every chassis moved oldest-first. And you watched it live from the dashboard, not from a Monday-morning Excel.

Phase 3 · Desk 2 · workshop & bodyshop — live from month 2

The service lane: job card to gate pass

Sales sells the first car; service sells every next one. Same discipline, applied to the workshop.

Job card opens Service advisor

Reg no, km, service type (PMS / running repair / warranty / accessories), customer complaints, the accessory checklist (spare wheel, tools, stereo — signed before the car enters), pick-and-drop details.

Estimate approved Customer

The advisor records the estimate and the customer's approval before work starts — the end of "nobody told me it would cost this much".

Labour & parts Advisor / technician stock auto-deducts

Each labour line and part added to the card. Parts pull from the spares inventory live — overselling a part is impossible, and stock is always the truth.

Offers & documents Advisor

A service coupon (say, monsoon-check ₹500 off) applies before GST with its own limits. Then the paperwork, each a numbered document: proforma invoice (estimate), cash memo (retail), debit memo (billed to insurer), parts requisition — snapshots that can never silently change after issue.

Invoice & gate pass Billing

18% GST computed, invoice numbered, vehicle delivered with a gate pass. The advisor's revenue and labour share land on the workshop dashboard by name.

Bodyshop claims Bodyshop coordinator

Insurance jobs run a 15-stage lifecycle — estimate, surveyor, DO, repair, BI/BR invoices — with the insurer-vs-customer split tracked to the rupee. A live register shows exactly what each insurer still owes you.

Phase 3 · Desks 3–5 · the revenue you're leaking today

Spares counter · Insurance renewals · True Value

Spares: counter sale & reorder Parts manager

Retail counter billing with GST, purchase GRN updating stock, and a suggestive order screen: every part at or below minimum, with suggested quantity and order value — export and send to your supplier. No more stock-outs discovered mid-service.

Insurance: the renewal engine CRE team targets & conveyance

Every policy nearing expiry becomes a case. The manager assigns cases to CREs, sets monthly targets, and watches conversion live — renewed premium, lost reasons, ₹-per-renewal conveyance. At MAPL this engine sits on a book of 90,000 policies.

True Value: used cars with discipline Evaluator → approver

Evaluation → purchase price approved (maker–checker) → refurb costs tracked in buckets (bodyshop / workshop / other) → sale with margin-scheme GST computed correctly. Every deal shows its true net P&L; no more "we think we made money on that Swift".

Phase 3 · Desk 6 · accounts — live from week 4

Accounts: books that close themselves

Most of your accounting has already happened — invoices, receipts and refunds posted their own entries. What's left is the accountant's real work.

Day book & vouchers Accountant maker → checker

Journal entries with live Dr/Cr balancing — an unbalanced voucher cannot be saved, an unapproved one cannot post to ledgers.

Daily cash & petty cash Cashier

Denomination-wise cash sheet (2000s down to coins) with computed shortage; petty cash requests routed through approval with a disbursement step.

Bank reconciliation Accountant

Paste the bank statement CSV, tick off matches, see the unreconciled gap shrink to zero.

TDS & outstanding Accounts head

Vendor TDS computed by section (194C, 194J…) with thresholds, deposit tracking and a section-wise register. Receivables and payables aged 0–30–60–90 by party.

GST returns Your CA portal-ready JSON

GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B generated from the sale register itself — B2B, B2C, HSN summary, document range — and exported in the government offline-tool JSON your CA uploads directly. The totals reconcile to the register to the paisa, because they come from it.

Financial statements Dealer principal

Trial balance, P&L and balance sheet on demand, drilled by group — not quarter-end archaeology. At MAPL these tie to ₹2,134 crore against the migrated ledgers.

Phase 3 · Desk 7 · people — live from month 3

HR & payroll: from punch to payslip

Attendance & leave Everyone

Daily attendance (with a mobile punch from any phone), leave requests with approvals, mispunch corrections through maker–checker.

Payroll run HR statutory built-in

One click computes the month from structures + attendance: PF 12%, ESI, professional tax, TDS under both regimes — payslips generated, bank-transfer sheet exported.

Incentives HR → Accounts

Sales and workshop incentives with a dual approval chain (HR then accounts) before payout — MAPL runs 7,874 of these through it.

Exits, loans, recruitment HR

Staged exit (self → HOD → HR → CEO) with automatic full-and-final math; staff loans with EMI schedules; a candidate pipeline that onboards straight into the employee master.
