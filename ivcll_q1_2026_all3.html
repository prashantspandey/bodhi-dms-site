Why margins are higher than road logistics
Indian Railways subsidizes automobile freight at ₹2.47/km/car — 5× cheaper than road.
IVC captures the spread: Maruti pays ₹24,649/car, IVC's direct cost is ₹12,363/car.
This ₹12,286/car spread on 4,871 cars = ₹5.98 Cr gross contribution.
Dedicated OEM railway contracts with owned rakes are among the most profitable logistics models in India precisely because of IR subsidy.
