Why revenue is estimated, not extracted from DB
DB limitation: The ChallanCN table that links railway challans to revenue CNs contains only 2014 test data — the link is broken. Without it, we cannot map a specific railway dispatch (IndentRequest) to its exact revenue CNs.
What we tried: (1) SurCharge > 0 pool — captured only the amortisation layer (~5% of actual). (2) BECH/TL/ KM>1500 pool — captured only long-haul cars, missing the medium-distance dealer deliveries. Both underestimate.
External estimate used: Maruti Suzuki typically pays ₹35,000–₹50,000/car for multimodal (rail+road) from Bechraji/Manesar to dealers across India. We use ₹40,000/car as a conservative midpoint.
Costs are fully DB-confirmed from IVC.dbo.tblt_R_IncidentalExpenseDetails. All shared-RR bills distributed by closest-load-date logic. Duplicate bills removed.
