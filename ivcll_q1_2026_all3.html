Why the margin is 44% gross — and what true EBITDA looks like
The 44% gross margin is on direct variable costs only: IR haulage + loading + unloading charges. These are real out-of-pocket payments IVC makes per dispatch — confirmed from the DB.

What's not in the DB costs:
  • Rake EMI / depreciation: Each IVCLL rake ~₹14–20 Cr capital. On 15-yr finance at 9%, that's ~₹1.5–2 Cr/rake/year in EMI. For 3 rakes per quarter: ₹1.1–1.5 Cr.
  • Staff: 50–100 people in IVC's railway division (rake in-charges, loading supervisors, yard staff at 10+ locations). Say ₹60–80L per quarter for the division.
  • Maintenance & repairs: Brake tests, wheel checks, oil, periodic overhaul — ₹30–50L per rake per year = ₹22–37L for 3 rakes per quarter.
  • Insurance: Cargo + rake hull — ₹5–10L per quarter for 3 rakes.
Estimated fixed cost for 3 rakes Q1: ₹2–3 Cr

True EBITDA estimate:
Revenue (DB rates): ₹10.81 Cr  −  Variable cost: ₹6.02 Cr  −  Fixed est. ₹2.5 Cr  =  ₹229L  ·  ~21% EBITDA
This is consistent with dedicated auto-rail logistics industry (typical 10–25% EBITDA for captive OEM contracts).
