How a Railway Dispatch Works — Step by Step Real Example: RR 282000043 · Maruti Suzuki · Feb–Aug 2026
0
Rate Contract (Before Everything Begins)
Pre-agreed · No date
IVC and the OEM (e.g. Maruti Suzuki) sign a rate contract that defines exactly how much IVC will charge per vehicle per route. This contract is stored in IVC's Rate Master. Without this, no order can be placed.
DB Proof: RateMaster table — route "Mundra Port → Manesar", rate locked at ₹87,853 per consignment
No money moves at this stage Agreed rate enables all future billing
1
Shipment Order (Customer Places Order)
4 Feb 2026
Maruti Suzuki places an order with IVC to transport imported cars from Mundra Port (Gujarat) to their Manesar plant. IVC raises a Consignment Note (CN) — the official billing document for this shipment.
DB Proof: CNMaster — CN No. GUR349913 · Consignor: Maruti Suzuki India Ltd. · Origin: Mundra Port · Dest: Manesar · Date: 4 Feb 2026
IVC earns: ₹87,853 (CN GUR349913) Cash collected only after POD (Step 8)
2
Railway Indent (IVC Books a Rake)
Before 7 Feb 2026
IVC files an Indent Request with Indian Railways to reserve rake IVCLL-07 for the Sabarmati → Manesar journey. Indian Railways confirms and assigns the rake and siding (loading track).
DB Proof: tblt_R_IndentRequest — ID 2778 · Indent No. 24.04 · Rake: IVCLL-07 · Office: Sabarmati
No payment yet — commitment only
3
Loading (Cars Put on Train)
7 Feb 2026 (Sabarmati) · 28 Mar 2026 (Manesar)
Cars are physically driven onto flatcar wagons at the railway yard by a loading contractor's crew. Each car is secured on the flatcar wagon. Feb leg (Sabarmati): Rake IVCLL-07 placed on siding at 6:10 AM, loading completed 10:00 AM — no separate contractor bill recorded. Mar leg (Manesar): RKM Enterprises loaded 264 cars on IVCLL-09.
DB Proof (Feb leg): datIR_PlaceDate = 7 Feb 6:10 AM · datIR_LoadDate = 7 Feb 10:00 AM · DB Proof (Mar leg): Bill #2223 — RKM Enterprises, 264 cars @ ₹245
Feb leg loading: No separate bill (included in IR contract) Mar leg loading — RKM Enterprises: ₹64,680
4
Main Dispatch (Train Departs, RR Issued)
7 Feb 2026 · After 10:00 AM
Indian Railways issues the Railway Receipt (RR) — the official freight document, like a bill of lading for trains. The train departs. IVC is now liable to pay Indian Railways the haulage charges.
DB Proof: txtIR_RRNo = 282000043 · IR Bill: 9926022472420018 (Feb leg) · IR Bill: 9926030638250225 (Mar leg)
IVC pays IR (Feb leg, Sabarmati→Manesar): ₹3,15,315 IVC pays IR (Mar leg, Manesar→Doddaballapur): ₹37,44,003
5
Transit (Train Travels to Destination)
7 Feb → 7 Feb (5 hrs) · 28 Mar → 4 Apr (7 days)
The train travels the route. Tracked via Railway Receipt number. No loading/unloading during transit. Goods trains in India average 25–50 km/h. The Mar 28 leg (Manesar→Doddaballapur, ~2,100 km) took exactly 7 days. The Feb 7 leg (Sabarmati→Manesar, ~950 km) would take 19–38 hours at typical goods train speeds — the same-day ReachDate in the CN likely reflects last-mile truck delivery timing, not train arrival.
DB Proof (Mar leg): CN 263420176 ReachDate = 4 Apr 2026, 10:29 AM · Departed 28 Mar 4 PM → Arrived ~4 Apr = 7 days · ~300 km/day average
₹0 — cost already included in haulage (Step 4)
6
Unloading (Cars Taken Off Train)
7 Feb 2026 · 2:59 PM & 5 Apr 2026
Cars are unloaded from wagons at the destination railway yard by local unloading contractors. IVC's field employee supervises the process. Contractors bill IVC per car unloaded.
DB Proof: Bill #2529 — Sanathkumar K R. Enterprises, DBU (Doddaballapur), 264 cars @ ₹275 · Anup Kumar Singh fooding "dtd.5/4/26" (Bill #2447)
IVC pays unloading contractor: ₹72,600 Employee fooding: ₹300
7
Last Mile Delivery (Cars Driven to Dealers)
May 2026 – Aug 2026
After unloading at the railway yard, individual cars are driven by IVC drivers to the actual dealer showrooms or OEM plants. This is the "last mile" — often a few kilometres from the yard. Drivers claim fuel reimbursements which are often filed late.
DB Proof: Bill #2680 (₹529 · May 7) · Bill #2694 (₹529 · May 7) · Bill #3645 (₹529 · Aug 17 — filed 5 months late, MDA 33)
Fuel reimbursements (3 drivers): ₹1,587 total Bill #3645 = the ₹529 filed 5 months late — our starting point
8
POD — Proof of Delivery (Cash Collected)
13 Feb 2026 (Manesar leg)
The receiving party (Maruti Suzuki plant, dealer) signs the POD confirming cars received in good condition. This is the trigger for IVC to collect payment. Without a signed POD, IVC cannot raise a final invoice.
DB Proof: CNMaster — CN GUR349913 · PODDate = 13 Feb 2026
IVC collects from Maruti Suzuki: ₹87,853 GST (18%) adds ₹15,813 → Total invoice: ₹1,03,666

Road CN vs Railway CN — What's the Difference? Both use CNMaster table · Different CNType

A CN (Consignment Note) is IVC's billing document for every vehicle movement — whether by truck or by train. Both live in the same CNMaster table in the database, but they work very differently.

🚛 Road CN (Truck Delivery) — CNType = 4
Transport
One truck, one trip
Capacity
6–12 cars per truck (car carrier)
Booking
1 CN per truck trip
Vehicle No.
Truck registration no. (e.g. HR55AK0012)
Driver
Named truck driver, mobile number tracked
Linked to
TripLog → Hire Slip (TLHSNO like MNSR/TL/…)
Typical freight
₹5,000–₹15,000 per CN
Transit time
Hours to 2–3 days
GPS tracked
Yes — real-time via GPS device
Real example
CN 271310724 · Kharkhoda · ₹6,267 · Truck MNSR/TL/1192534
🚂 Railway CN — CNType = 3
Transport
One rake (train), many wagons
Capacity
100–264 cars per rake dispatch
Booking
1 CN per rake (covers all cars)
Vehicle No.
Rake number (e.g. IVCLL-09) + RR Number
Driver
No driver — Indian Railways crew operates train
Linked to
ChallanCN → RlyChallan → IndentRequest → RR Number
Typical freight
₹50,000–₹2,00,000 per CN (bulk)
Transit time
4–10 days (long distance)
GPS tracked
No — tracked via IR Railway Receipt
